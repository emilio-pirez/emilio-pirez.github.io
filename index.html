Custom Thematic Portfolios: Genetic Algorithm Guided by Risk Profiling

Undergraduate thesis · Python · Team of 3 · 2026

A client answers a risk-profiling questionnaire and picks the themes they want (sectors, styles, sizes). Their answers become an explicit, auditable objective function, and a genetic algorithm builds a 20–35 stock portfolio from the full U.S. market that respects the client's risk limits out of sample.

94%of quarters within all client risk limits
66%same-theme ETF mix, same test
55%Markowitz max-Sharpe, same test
23% → 12%turnover, with no return loss
  • Hard risk ceilings on beta, tracking error, maximum drawdown and CVaR, anchored to industry reference levels.
  • Long-only, ~4,900 U.S. stocks, sector/style/size constraints; data from FMP and FRED APIs.
  • Quarterly walk-forward backtest 2016–2026 with transaction costs and taxes; parameters calibrated only on 2010–2015.
